# Email Templates

## Unit director approval email

```text
Subject: <Unit name> (<unit code>) hours summary

Dear <Name>,

Could I please ask you to confirm that my teaching and marking hours to date for <Unit name> (<unit code>), <Department>, come to <total> hours in total?

Breakdown:

- <Activity 1>: <hours>
  <Short rationale>

- <Activity 2>: <hours>
  <Short rationale>

- <Activity 3>: <hours>
  <Short rationale>

A short reply confirming that these hours are accurate would be greatly appreciated, so I can record the authorisation details for the hours-claim system.

Many thanks,
<User>
```

## Colleague explanation

```text
I worked this out using the actual number of sessions/scripts together with the course lead's rough marking estimates where available.

The basic formula was:

total time = fixed time + marking time + entry/checking time

For taught or support sessions, I used:

number of sessions x (class time + prep time)

For meetings or training, I used:

actual duration

For partial marking by script range, I used:

full-sheet allocation x (scripts handled / total scripts)

For formative sheets without a better split, I used:

1h + (number of submissions x 5min) + 0.5h

and then adjusted slightly depending on whether I handled only one question, did temporary scores, or did the final mark entry.
```
